# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Redialer** app handles your information.

## No Data Collection
The Redialer app **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**. All information processed by the app stays exclusively on your device. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking services.

## Use of Permissions
To provide its functionality, the app requires access to certain sensitive permissions. These are used strictly for the app's core features:

### 1. Read Contacts (`android.permission.READ_CONTACTS`)
This permission is used **only** when you manually choose to pick a contact from your address book. The app reads the selected contact's name and phone number to fill in the redialing fields. No contact data is ever uploaded or shared.

### 2. Make Phone Calls (`android.permission.CALL_PHONE`)
This permission is essential for the app's primary purpose: redialing. It allows the app to initiate outgoing calls to the phone number you have specified.

### 3. Read Phone State (`android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE`)
This permission is used to monitor the status of your phone calls. Specifically, it allows the app to detect when a call has ended (transitioning back to an idle state). This detection is necessary to trigger the next redial attempt after the configured delay.

## Data Sharing
Since no personal data is collected, **no data is shared** with any third parties, companies, or individuals.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the GitHub repository.
